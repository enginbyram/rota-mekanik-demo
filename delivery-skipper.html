Delivery & Skipper

We move your boat from one marina to another, or join you on board as skipper.

Request a QuoteCall us
Image · Yacht under way along the coast
Overview

Your boat, moved safely between marinas.

We plan the route and the weather, check the boat before departure and deliver it on the agreed date, with a handover report on arrival. If the weather is wrong, we wait.

For
Owners moving the boat or wanting a skipper
Boats
Motor yachts and sailboats from 7 m
Routes
Between marinas on the Turkish coast
Reporting
Handover report on arrival
Scope

What is included

Everything outside this list is quoted separately and starts only with your approval.

Included
  • Route, weather and preparation plan
  • General condition check before departure
  • Delivery on the agreed route and date
  • Basic skipper support under way
  • Handover report after delivery
  • Clarifying crew needs
  • Advice to delay or cancel for weather
Not included (quoted separately)
  • Commercial commitment before qualifications, insurance and permits are clear
  • Seaworthiness guarantee
  • Fuel, marina, port, customs and transit fees
  • Commercial passenger transport, charter and tourism
  • International delivery before permits, insurance and flag are in place
  • Permanent crew, maintenance team or security staff
  • Sailing against the weather on the owner’s instruction

FAQ

Common questions

Where can you deliver?

Between marinas on the Turkish coast. International deliveries need permits, insurance and flag requirements in place first.

Do you provide a skipper?

Yes. We can deliver the boat or join you on board as skipper.

What happens if the weather turns?

We advise a delay or cancellation. We do not sail against the weather.

What does the price include?

Planning, delivery and a handover report. Fuel, marina, port and customs fees are extra.

Away from your boat?

Tell us the boat, its length and the marina. We reply with a written scope and a quote.
