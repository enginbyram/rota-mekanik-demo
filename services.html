Boat Watch, Boat Care, Yacht Management and Delivery for yachts and vessels. We organise and follow up; you get one contact.

Boat Watch

Regular checks while you are away, extra visits before and after storms, and a photo report every time.

  • Visual check inside and out
  • Lines, fenders and covers
  • Bilge, damp and ventilation
  • Batteries, shore power and engine start
  • Extra visits before and after storms

We organise cleaning and maintenance and follow every job to the end.

  • Trusted technicians, found and followed
  • Engine, battery, electrical and sail needs tracked
  • Winter and season preparation
  • Maintenance list with an estimated budget

Delivery & Skipper

We move your boat from one marina to another, or join you on board as skipper.

  • Skipper support under way
