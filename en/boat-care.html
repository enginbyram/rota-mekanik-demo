We organise cleaning and maintenance, find trusted technicians, follow the work and prepare your boat for winter and season.

Overview

Maintenance organised and followed, start to finish.

We track what your boat needs, find the right technician and follow the job to the end. Their fees and parts are passed on at cost, on separate lines. We charge only our service fee.

For
Owners who want one team to run the upkeep
Boats
Motor yachts and sailboats from 7 m
Area
Mediterranean and Aegean coasts
Costs
Technicians and parts billed at cost
Scope

What is included

Everything outside this list is quoted separately and starts only with your approval.

Included
  • Cleaning, organised or done by us as agreed
  • Tracking engine, battery, electrical, plumbing and sail needs
  • Finding trusted technicians and following the work
  • Winter and season preparation plan
  • Maintenance list with an estimated budget
Not included (quoted separately)
  • Detailed cleaning, paint, polish; cleaning materials
  • Doing maintenance and repairs ourselves
  • Technicians’ fees, spare parts and their warranty
  • Winter storage, haul-out, marina and storage fees
  • Insurance claims and legal matters

FAQ

Common questions

Do you do the repairs yourselves?

We organise and follow the work. Trusted technicians do the technical work.

How are technicians paid?

Their fees and parts are passed on at cost, on separate lines. We charge only our service fee.

What if the job grows?

Anything outside the written scope needs your approval first.

Can you prepare my boat for winter?

Yes. We plan winter and season preparation and organise the work.

Away from your boat?

Tell us the boat, its length and the marina. We reply with a written scope and a quote.
