Boat Watch

Regular checks while you are away, extra visits before and after storms, and a photo report every time.

Overview

Your boat, checked while you are away.

We visit your boat on a set schedule, check it inside and out, and send you photos and notes after every visit. If something needs work, we tell you first and organise it only with your approval.

For
Owners who leave the boat for long periods
Boats
Motor yachts and sailboats from 7 m
Area
Mediterranean and Aegean coasts
Reporting
Photo and video report after every visit
Scope

What is included

Everything outside this list is quoted separately and starts only with your approval.

Included
  • Visual check inside and out
  • Lines, fenders and covers
  • Bilge, damp, ventilation and odour
  • Batteries and shore power; engine start
  • Tank levels and visible leaks
  • Extra checks before and after storms
  • Photo or video report and visit log
Not included (quoted separately)
  • Technical measurement, testing and fault finding
  • Replacing or buying lines, fenders or covers
  • Bilge cleaning, pumping out or drying
  • Battery, electrical or engine maintenance and repair
  • Refuelling, water, tank cleaning and leak repair
  • Standing by the boat during a storm
  • Guarantee against hidden defects; moving the boat

FAQ

Common questions

How often do you visit?

Two or four times a month, depending on the plan, plus extra visits before and after storms.

What is in the report?

Photos or video of the boat inside and out, and notes on anything that needs attention.

Do you repair what you find?

We report it and, with your approval, organise a trusted technician through Boat Care.

How is the price set?

By boat type and length: motor yacht or sailboat, 7–15 m or over 15 m. Ask for a quote.

Away from your boat?

Tell us the boat, its length and the marina. We reply with a written scope and a quote.
